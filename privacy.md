# VR Survival Zombies — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026
**Developer:** Pland ("we", "us")

VR Survival Zombies is a free virtual reality game for Meta Quest headsets. It has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no voice chat. This policy explains the little data the game handles and why.

## 1. Data the game does not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, age, location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera images or any account information. The game requests no access to the microphone, camera, storage or location of your headset.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

## 2. Online co-op (Photon)

The two-player online mode uses **Photon Fusion**, a multiplayer service operated by Exit Games GmbH, to connect players and relay the match between their headsets. Our server region is the United States.

When you play online, Photon necessarily processes:

- your **IP address**, to establish the network connection;
- a **temporary session identifier** generated for that match;
- **game-state data** for the match itself: player positions and movements, actions such as shooting or grabbing objects, and match events such as waves and scores.

This data is used only to run the match while you play. We do not store it on any server of our own. Photon's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: <https://www.photonengine.com/privacy>.

Rooms are listed publicly to other players running the same version of the game, so that a partner can find and join your match.

**Solo Survival mode works fully offline** and sends no data at all.

## 3. Data stored on your headset

The game saves two pieces of information **locally on your headset** only:

- your **personal best score** in Solo Survival;
- your **standing height**, as measured by the headset's tracking, so that your in-game body is sized correctly the next time you play.

This data never leaves your device: it is not sent to us, to Photon or to any other service, and your partner in co-op never sees your height (only the resulting avatar). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## 4. Meta platform

The game is distributed through Meta's platforms and runs on Meta Quest hardware. Data that Meta collects through its headsets, accounts and store is governed by Meta's own privacy policy: <https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/>.

## 5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Because the game collects no personal data itself, there is nothing for us to store or delete; if you have a concern, contact us at the address above.

## 6. Your rights and questions

Since we keep no personal data on our side, there is no profile for us to access, correct or delete. The only data the game keeps is the local data described in section 3, and you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game from your headset. For any question about this policy or about data handled by the online mode, write to the GitHub repository owner's email and we will answer.

## 7. Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, adding voice chat or analytics), we will update this page and its effective date before releasing that version.
